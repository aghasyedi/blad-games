# Quantum Arcade

Forty playable quantum games - chess, battleship, ludo, carrom, cricket and more - in a hand-drawn neon brutalist arcade. Pure HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Every piece is in two places at once until you measure it.

## The Games

1. **Quantum Chess** (Strategy) - Chess where pieces exist in superposition and only become definite after measurement. Uses superposition, entanglement, interference and quantum moves.
2. **Quantum Battleship** (Strategy) - Ships occupy multiple possible positions until the opponent measures a location. Balance information gathering against destroying hidden quantum ships.
3. **Quantum Tic-Tac-Toe** (Board) - Players place quantum moves that exist in multiple squares at once. Entanglement and measurement decide when the board collapses into a classical state.
4. **Quantum Maze** (Puzzle) - Explore multiple maze paths at once through a simulated quantum walk. Interference can raise or lower the probability of reaching particular locations.
5. **Quantum Escape Room** (Puzzle) - Solve puzzles by applying quantum gates such as X, H, Z and CNOT. Each level teaches one quantum concept through an interactive challenge.
6. **Quantum Tower Defense** (Strategy) - Enemies split into multiple possible paths instead of following one fixed route. Quantum towers manipulate, measure or interfere with these possible states.
7. **Qubit Duel** (Strategy) - Two players control qubits and use gates to attack, defend or manipulate states. The winner is decided by strategic measurement and state manipulation.
8. **Quantum Card Game** (Cards) - Cards represent superpositions of different values or suits. Players decide when to manipulate or measure cards while managing uncertainty.
9. **Quantum Dice** (Arcade) - A quantum die exists as a probability distribution over several outcomes. Players choose when to measure and can use quantum operations to alter probabilities.
10. **Quantum Rock-Paper-Scissors** (Arcade) - Rock-Paper-Scissors extended with quantum strategies and superposition. Delay measurement and exploit interference to influence outcomes.
11. **Quantum Dungeon** (Strategy) - Navigate a dungeon where locations, enemies and objects exist in superposition. Measurement and quantum gates become strategic abilities during exploration.
12. **Quantum Spy** (Puzzle) - A spy hides information inside quantum states while an opponent tries to discover it. Measurement reveals information but may also disturb or destroy the hidden state.
13. **Quantum Minesweeper** (Puzzle) - Mines initially exist in uncertain or superposed locations. Players use measurements to reveal information while minimising dangerous observations.
14. **Qubit Pong** (Arcade) - The ball's quantum state determines multiple possible trajectories. Measurements collapse the possibilities, creating unpredictable but controllable gameplay.
15. **Quantum Tetris** (Arcade) - Falling blocks have multiple possible orientations or positions before measurement. Manipulate the quantum state to create favourable board configurations.
16. **Quantum Carrom** (Indian Classics) - The striker has multiple possible trajectories before interacting with coins. Superposition, interference and measurement determine collisions and pocketing outcomes.
17. **Quantum Kabaddi** (Indian Classics) - A raider can occupy multiple possible positions while entering the opponent's territory. Defenders use measurement and quantum operations to detect or intercept the raider.
18. **Quantum Kho-Kho** (Indian Classics) - Players exist across multiple possible positions on the playing field. Quantum movement, teleportation and measurement create new chasing strategies.
19. **Quantum Ludo** (Indian Classics) - The dice and token movement are represented using quantum probability states. Players can manipulate probabilities before measuring the final dice outcome.
20. **Quantum Pachisi** (Indian Classics) - Traditional Pachisi redesigned so tokens can occupy multiple possible board positions. Measurement collapses token locations and creates new strategic possibilities.
21. **Quantum Pallanguzhi** (Indian Classics) - The traditional seed-and-pit game redesigned with quantum states for seed distributions. Entanglement and measurement decide how resources move between pits.
22. **Quantum Teen Patti** (Indian Classics) - Cards exist as superpositions of different ranks or suits before being measured. Players combine quantum uncertainty with bluffing, probability and risk management.
23. **Quantum Snakes & Ladders** (Indian Classics) - Players can occupy multiple possible squares instead of one fixed position. Quantum ladders, snakes and measurements determine how the player's state evolves.
24. **Quantum Raja-Mantri-Chor-Sipahi** (Indian Classics) - Hidden roles are represented as quantum states rather than immediately known identities. Players use incomplete information, measurement and AI strategies to identify opponents.
25. **Quantum Checkers** (Board) - Checkers pieces occupy superpositions of multiple squares and become definite after measurement. Use quantum moves, interference and entanglement to create unexpected captures.
26. **Quantum Go** (Board) - Stones can exist in multiple possible board positions before measurement. Manipulate quantum territories and use interference to influence which regions become occupied.
27. **Quantum Connect Four** (Board) - A placed piece can exist in multiple possible columns until measurement. Entangled pieces and quantum moves create branching board states players must strategically collapse.
28. **Quantum Monopoly** (Board) - Properties, player positions and even dice outcomes exist as probability distributions. Use quantum operations to manipulate movement and ownership before measurement.
29. **Quantum Car Racing** (Arcade) - A car can follow multiple possible racing lines simultaneously. Use quantum gates and measurements to influence trajectories, overtaking opportunities and race outcomes.
30. **Quantum Football** (Sports) - Players and the ball occupy multiple possible positions on the field. Quantum passes, superposition and measurement create unpredictable attacking and defensive strategies.
31. **Quantum Cricket** (Sports) - A batsman's shot exists as multiple possible trajectories while the ball has quantum uncertainty. Manipulate probabilities to influence runs, wickets and fielding outcomes.
32. **Quantum Badminton** (Sports) - The shuttlecock has multiple possible trajectories before measurement. Use quantum positioning, interference and strategic measurements to create deceptive shots.
33. **Quantum Tennis** (Sports) - Ball trajectories and player positions exist in superposition. Decide when to measure and collapse possible rallies while using quantum operations to alter probabilities.
34. **Quantum Snooker** (Sports) - Balls exist in multiple possible positions and trajectories before measurement. Manipulate quantum states to create favourable combinations and pocketing opportunities.
35. **Quantum Battleships: Fleet War** (Strategy) - Multiple ships exist across superposed positions and become definite only after measurements or attacks. Balance probing, probability manipulation and strategic attacks to locate the fleet.
36. **Quantum Sudoku** (Puzzle) - Numbers initially occupy multiple possible cells or values simultaneously. Quantum constraints, measurements and entanglement let players solve puzzles while controlling when uncertainty collapses.
37. **Quantum Crossword** (Puzzle) - Letters exist as superpositions of possible answers until clues or measurements resolve them. Correct quantum constraints can collapse several uncertain words simultaneously.
38. **Quantum Memory** (Puzzle) - Matching cards exist in uncertain or entangled states rather than having fixed identities. Measure cards to gather information while trying to preserve useful quantum correlations.
39. **Quantum Treasure Hunt** (Puzzle) - Treasure and clues exist in multiple possible locations simultaneously. Perform measurements and quantum operations to narrow the search while avoiding misleading collapses.
40. **Quantum Indian Treasure Hunt** (Indian Classics) - A multiplayer treasure hunt inspired by Indian landmarks, mythology and puzzles. Explore quantum versions of multiple possible locations using measurement, entanglement and interference to find the final treasure.
