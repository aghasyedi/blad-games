HOW IT WORKS

⚛

Superposition

Pieces, ships, dice and cards sit in two or more places at once. The board shows you every possibility as a ghost - only one of them is real.

🔗

Entanglement

Linked pieces move together. Touch one and its partner collapses too, which is either a gift or a disaster depending on where you left it.

📡

Interference

Some zones amplify your probability and some cancel it out. Route through the bright ones and your odds double.

🔭

Measurement

Measuring collapses the uncertainty into one definite answer. It is powerful, it is free, and it always hands information to the other player.

📱

Built for phones

Tap and swipe everywhere, 44px touch targets, no hover-only actions, DPR-aware canvases, and it remembers your progress between visits.

🔨

Zero dependencies

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No build step, no frameworks, no server. Open index.html and the whole arcade runs.
